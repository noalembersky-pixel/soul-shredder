Privacy

Soul Shredder · Last updated 19 November 2026

Soul Shredder is a pixel-art skateboarding game about a grim reaper who quits his job. This page explains what the game does and doesn't do with your information. It's short, because there isn't much to say.

What stays on your device

Soul Shredder has no accounts and no server of its own. It remembers a few things on your own device: your best Level 1 score, which levels you've finished (so the next one unlocks), and whether you turned the sound off. None of this is tied to you, and clearing your browser data (or the app's data) removes it.

Anonymous usage statistics

To learn whether the levels are too hard or too easy, we count simple events: a level was started, won or lost, a run was left part-way (through the menu, or by putting the game away), a Level 2 lesson or Level 3 session was reached, a locked level was tapped, a comic was skipped, and the share button was tapped. Each event carries only the level number and gameplay numbers (score, demons slashed, tricks, hits taken, tries, falls and seconds played). Nothing you type and nothing personal is recorded.

For this we use PostHog, an analytics service (PostHog Inc., data stored in the United States). PostHog receives the type of device and browser and an approximate location worked out from your IP address. The web game keeps no identifier between visits, so each play looks like a new visitor. The app stores one random ID on your phone so we can tell that the same phone played again. It is not linked to your name, your account, your advertising ID or any other app, and clearing the app's data resets it.

We do not use cookies, we do not record your screen, and we do not track you across other websites or apps. You can block the statistics with any content blocker; the game plays the same without them.

Sharing

The share button uses your phone's own share sheet to pass along the game's web address and a picture from the game. We don't see who you share it with.

Hosting

The web version is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like any web host, GitHub may log the IP address of visitors. See GitHub's privacy statement.

App stores

If you install Soul Shredder from Google Play or the App Store, Google or Apple may send us anonymous crash reports and basic install statistics under their own privacy policies. We do not receive your name or contact details from them.

What we don't do

We do not sell or share your information. We do not show ads and there are no purchases. We do not ask for your name, email, contacts, location, camera or microphone.

Children

Soul Shredder is a general-audience game. It collects no personal information from anyone, of any age.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date above.
