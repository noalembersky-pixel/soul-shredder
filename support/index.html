Escape hell. Shred the earth.

Soul Shredder is a pixel-art skateboarding game told like a comic. Most questions are answered below. If yours isn't, we're one email away.

How do I play?

Level 1, Escape from Hell: drag on the left side of the screen to fly. Slash swings the scythe at the demon catchers. Trick does a flip; tap again to chain tricks for more points. Dodge the lost souls (they can't be slashed), and grab wheels to fill your soul fire for Shred Mode.

Level 2, Learn to Skate: five street skaters each show you a skill, then it's your turn. Push with the stick, Ollie in the green zone, and tap again in the air for the trick. Unlimited tries.

Level 3, The Contest: three timed sessions. Ollie, kickflip and chain tricks over piles, stairs, rails and gaps to beat the target score.

On a computer: arrow keys to move, Space to slash or ollie, Shift for tricks. Each level unlocks when you finish the one before it.

Does it save my progress?

Yes, on your own device only: which levels you've finished, your best Level 1 score and your sound setting. There are no accounts, so clearing your browser data (or deleting the app) resets everything.

There's no sound.

Check the sound button on the title screen. Phones won't play audio until you tap something, so sound starts after your first tap. If it's still silent, check your phone's silent switch and volume.

The game looks tiny or squashed.

Soul Shredder plays in landscape, so turn your phone sideways. For the biggest screen in a browser, add it to your home screen (Share → Add to Home Screen) so the browser bar disappears.

How do I pause or go back to the menu?

Tap the pause button at the top of the screen. From there you can resume, go back to the menu, or share the game. The game also pauses by itself when you leave it.

How do I contact you?

Email support@oatmealmorning.com. We're a two-person studio, so you'll get a real person, usually within a few days. Bugs, high scores and trick ideas all welcome.
