Privacy

Soul Shredder · Last updated 24 November 2026

Soul Shredder is a pixel-art skateboarding game for iPhone and Android about a grim reaper who quits his job. This page explains what the game does and doesn't do with your information. It's short, because there isn't much to say.

What stays on your phone

Soul Shredder has no accounts and no server of its own. The app remembers a few things on your own phone: your best Level 1 score, which levels you've finished (so the next one unlocks), and whether you turned the sound off. None of this is tied to you. Clearing the app's storage or deleting the app removes it (on Android, if your phone's backup is on, Android may restore it when you reinstall).

Anonymous usage statistics

To learn whether the levels are too hard or too easy, we count simple events: the game was opened or closed, a level was started, won or lost, a run was left part-way (through the menu or the back button, or by putting the game away), a Level 2 lesson or Level 3 session was reached, a locked level was tapped, a comic was skipped, and the share button was tapped. Each event carries only whether you play on Android or iPhone, the app version, the level number and gameplay numbers (score, demons slashed, tricks, hits taken, tries, falls and seconds played). Nothing you type and nothing personal is recorded.

For this we use PostHog, an analytics service (PostHog Inc., data stored in the United States). PostHog receives the type of phone and its system version. Like any internet connection, your IP address reaches PostHog's servers, but we've set it to be thrown away on arrival, so no location is stored. The app stores one random ID on your phone so we can tell that the same phone played again. It is not linked to your name, your account, your advertising ID or any other app, and clearing the app's data resets it.

We do not use cookies, we do not record your screen, and we do not track you across other websites or apps. The game plays the same, also offline, when the statistics can't be sent.

Sharing

The share button uses your phone's own share sheet to pass along a link to the game's page (where people can get it) and a picture from the game. We don't see who you share it with.

This website

soulshredder.oatmealmorning.com is the game's information page; the game itself is only in the app. When you open the home page, we count the visit with PostHog in the same way: the page, the type of device and browser, no cookies, no identifier, and the IP address thrown away on arrival. The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like any web host, GitHub may log the IP address of visitors. See GitHub's privacy statement.

App stores

If you install Soul Shredder from Google Play or the App Store, Google or Apple may send us anonymous crash reports and basic install statistics under their own privacy policies. We do not receive your name or contact details from them.

What we don't do

We do not sell or share your information. We do not show ads and there are no purchases. We do not ask for your name, email, contacts, location, camera or microphone.

Children

Soul Shredder is a general-audience game. It collects no personal information from anyone, of any age.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date above.
