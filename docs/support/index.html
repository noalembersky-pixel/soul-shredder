Escape hell. Shred the earth.

Soul Shredder is a pixel-art skateboarding game for iPhone and Android, told like a comic. Most questions are answered below. If yours isn't, we're one email away.

How do I play?

Level 1, Escape from Hell: drag on the left side of the screen to fly. Slash swings the scythe at the demon catchers. Trick does a flip; tap again to chain tricks for more points. Dodge the lost souls (they can't be slashed), and grab wheels to fill your soul fire for Shred Mode.

Level 2, Learn to Skate: five street skaters each show you a skill, then it's your turn. Push with the stick, Ollie in the green zone, and tap again in the air for the trick. Unlimited tries.

Level 3, The Contest: three timed sessions. Ollie, kickflip and chain tricks over piles, stairs, rails and gaps to beat the target score.

Each level unlocks when you finish the one before it.

Does it save my progress?

Yes, on your own phone only: which levels you've finished, your best Level 1 score and your sound setting. There are no accounts, so clearing the app's storage or deleting the app resets everything (on Android, your phone's backup may bring it back when you reinstall).

There's no sound.

Music starts on the title screen. If it's quiet, check the sound button on the title screen, then turn up your phone's volume. If the game shows TAP TO START, tap it once to start the sound. The game plays sound even when your phone is on silent; use the game's sound button to turn it off.

Which way do I hold my phone?

Sideways. Soul Shredder plays in landscape only, and either sideways direction works.

How do I pause or go back to the menu?

Tap the pause button at the top of the screen. From there you can resume, go back to the menu, or share the game. On Android, the back button pauses too. The game also pauses by itself when you leave it.

Where can I get Soul Shredder?

How do I contact you?

Email support@oatmealmorning.com. We're a two-person studio, so you'll get a real person, usually within a few days. Bugs, high scores and trick ideas all welcome.
