Description (1546/4000):
Meet Shred. Grim reaper. Soul collector since the dawn of time. Zero sick days, zero vacation, zero fun.

Then a skateboard falls into hell, Shred tries it once, and he is hooked. He tells the Devil he quits. The Devil says no. So Shred grabs his board and escapes.

Soul Shredder is a pixel-art skateboarding game told like a comic book. Three levels take Shred out of the fire pits of hell, through the streets of the living and into his first real contest.

LEVEL 1: ESCAPE FROM HELL
- Fly through hell on your board and slash the demons who come to catch you.
- Dodge lost souls and grab skate wheels to fill your soul fire.
- Chain kickflips, grabs, backflips and barrel rolls for big points.
- Fill the fire meter to unlock Shred Mode: a giant scythe and double points.

LEVEL 2: LEARN TO SKATE
Five street skaters each teach you one skill. They show you first, then it is your turn.
- Ollie a growing pile of boards.
- Kickflip down the stairs.
- Grind the rail and keep your balance.
- Hold a manual across the pad.
- Put it all together in one line over the big gap.

LEVEL 3: THE CONTEST
Your first contest, with a DJ and a crowd. Three timed sessions. Chain tricks over piles, stairs, rails and gaps to build combos and beat the score to win.

WHAT YOU GET
- Hand-made pixel art and illustrated comic cutscenes.
- Original chiptune music and sound effects.
- Simple touch controls: a stick to move and two buttons for tricks.
- Plays in landscape, fully offline.
- No ads, no accounts, no data collected.
